Animals

Welcome to Native NZ, an informative platform dedicated to the unique wildlife of Aotearoa. Our primary mission is to educate both locals and international visitors about the extraordinary animals that call New Zealand home—many of which are found nowhere else on Earth. Through this site, we aim to provide comprehensive insights into various native species, detailing their habitats, diets, and complex behavioural patterns, while also highlighting the critical dangers they face, such as introduced predators and habitat loss. Whether you are a tourist planning to see these treasures in person, a local looking to better protect your backyard, or a dedicated animal enthusiast, our goal is to inspire everyone to take an active role in conservation. By sharing clear, accessible information on population trends and safety, we hope to ensure these "taonga" (treasures) are preserved for generations to come.
