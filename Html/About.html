About Native Nz

Preserving the unique soul of Aotearoa through education

Our Primary Mission

Native NZ is a dedicated informative platform designed to showcase the extraordinary animals of Aotearoa.Our goal is to provide comprehensive insights into various native species, detailing their habitats, diets, and complex behavioral patterns

Beyond basic facts, we aim to inform the public about the critical dangers these animals face and why it is vital that we protect them. These creatures are found nowhere else on earth, making their survival a global priority.

Who is this for?

Tourists

New Zealanders can educate themselves on how to protect our "taonga" from extinction right in their own backyards.

Enthusiasts

Animal lovers can dive deep into technical details about behavioural patterns and population safety.
